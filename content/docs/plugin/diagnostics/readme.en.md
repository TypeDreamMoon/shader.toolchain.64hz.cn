# DreamShader diagnostics

Every `DSHnnnn` DreamShader can emit. The leading digit is the stage that raises it:

- **DSH1xxx** --- Driver, source files and imports --- [DSH1xxx.md](/en/docs/plugin/diagnostics/dsh1xxx)
- **DSH2xxx** --- Lexer and syntax --- [DSH2xxx.md](/en/docs/plugin/diagnostics/dsh2xxx)
- **DSH3xxx** --- Sections and declarations --- [DSH3xxx.md](/en/docs/plugin/diagnostics/dsh3xxx)
- **DSH4xxx** --- Graph statements and expressions --- [DSH4xxx.md](/en/docs/plugin/diagnostics/dsh4xxx)
- **DSH5xxx** --- Builtins -- UE.*, math, Substrate --- [DSH5xxx.md](/en/docs/plugin/diagnostics/dsh5xxx)
- **DSH6xxx** --- Functions and HLSL codegen --- [DSH6xxx.md](/en/docs/plugin/diagnostics/dsh6xxx)
- **DSH7xxx** --- Properties, parameters and settings --- [DSH7xxx.md](/en/docs/plugin/diagnostics/dsh7xxx)
- **DSH8xxx** --- Asset generation and saving --- [DSH8xxx.md](/en/docs/plugin/diagnostics/dsh8xxx)
- **DSH9xxx** --- Tools, sync and internal invariants --- [DSH9xxx.md](/en/docs/plugin/diagnostics/dsh9xxx)

Generated by `.skill/gen-diagnostics.ps1`; run it after adding a code.

[index.md](/en/docs/diagnostics) says where diagnostics appear and how to read one, maps the common messages of
the 1.x generator to the codes that replaced them, and lists the few messages that carry no code.
